Legal ops vendor contract triage (anonymized pilot)

Disclosure: Anonymized pilot illustration. Metrics from a 60-day evaluation at a growth-stage SaaS company. Individual results vary.

Profile

  • Team: 2 in-house attorneys + 1 legal ops analyst
  • Volume: ~180 vendor MSAs and order forms annually
  • Priority: Liability caps, DPA terms, auto-renewal, termination

Problem

Procurement routed all vendor paper to counsel regardless of risk. Counsel backlog delayed SaaS tool onboarding by 2–3 weeks for low-risk renewals mixed with high-risk new vendors.

Workflow change

  1. Legal ops runs liability and indemnity clauses through free checkers and full sandbox.
  2. Scores under 55: analyst marks up with playbook fallbacks. Scores 55–75: counsel review. Above 75: counsel + procurement escalation.
  3. Quarterly calibration against signed agreements.
