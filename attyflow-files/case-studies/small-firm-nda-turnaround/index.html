Small firm NDA turnaround (anonymized pilot)

Disclosure: Anonymized pilot workflow illustration based on typical customer patterns. Metrics are directional estimates from a structured 30-day evaluation — not guaranteed results. No client names or identifying details are disclosed.

Profile

  • Organization: 4-attorney US boutique, corporate transactional practice
  • Volume: ~35 mutual NDAs per month (incoming counterparty paper)
  • Tooling: Microsoft Word, Attyflow Solo sandbox → paid

Problem

Associates spent 35–50 minutes on first-pass NDA review before partner spot-check. Bottleneck: repetitive flagging of overbroad definitions, missing residuals carveouts, and one-way injunctive relief — the same issues on different counterparty templates.

Workflow change

  1. Associate pastes high-risk clauses into Attyflow with buyer/seller position set.
  2. Risk scores above 70 auto-escalate to partner queue with AI issue summary attached.
  3. Associate applies redline suggestions in Word; partner reviews flagged sections only.
