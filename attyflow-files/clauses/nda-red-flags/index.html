NDA red flags checklist

Use this checklist during first-pass NDA review. Pair with Attyflow's free NDA checker for instant triage.

High-risk patterns

  • Universal scope: "any and all information" without business-purpose limitation
  • No residuals: prohibits use of general knowledge retained in unaided memory
  • Perpetual term: confidentiality survives beyond reasonable period for non-trade-secrets
  • One-way injunctive relief: only receiving party bound to seek equitable remedies
  • Missing destruction: no certification of return or deletion

Negotiation fallbacks

Propose mutual definitions, 3–5 year term for non-trade-secret information, standard residuals carveout, and reciprocal injunctive relief language. Run proposed fallback through the sandbox with position set to your client side.
