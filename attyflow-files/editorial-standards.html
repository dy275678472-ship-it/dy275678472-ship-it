Editorial standards

How Attyflow publishes legal technology content responsibly.

Our principles

  • Not legal advice: All content supports software evaluation and workflow education. Qualified attorneys must review outputs for specific matters.
  • No fabricated proof: We do not publish fake testimonials, invented case studies, or unverified customer metrics.
  • Transparent comparisons: Competitor pages disclose Attyflow authorship and link to primary product pages.
  • Human review: Buying guides and clause libraries are edited for accuracy before publication.
  • Corrections: Factual errors reported to contact are corrected within 5 business days.

Content types

Product pages describe Attyflow capabilities as deployed. Tools and clause libraries provide educational checklists with links to the sandbox. Blog articles address legal ops workflows and may include product CTAs clearly labeled as software trials.

AI-assisted content

Some resources are drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by editors. Contract analysis inside the product requires attorney validation before reliance in negotiation.
