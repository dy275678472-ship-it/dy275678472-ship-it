NDA review workflow for attorneys

NDAs are high-volume, repetitive, and asymmetric — exactly where AI contract review delivers ROI. This use case shows how Attyflow fits into a typical mutual or one-way NDA review.

Common NDA risk areas

  • Overbroad definition of confidential information
  • Missing residual knowledge carveout
  • One-way injunctive relief without reciprocity
  • Indefinite confidentiality for non-trade-secret information
  • Weak return-or-destruction obligations

Recommended workflow

  1. Run the full NDA through your checklist or free NDA checker for flagged clauses.
  2. Paste high-risk sections into the sandbox with buyer/seller position set.
  3. Apply redline suggestions in Word; escalate scores above 75 to partner.
  4. Archive approved playbook positions for repeat counterparty paper.
